# SQLight

**Local-first AI SQL copilot for VS Code.**

Your schema never leaves your machine. SQLight runs on your own Ollama — no cloud AI, no data sent anywhere.

## What it does

- ⚡ **Schema-aware autocomplete** — knows your tables, columns, foreign keys, and types. Suggests real identifiers, not random keywords.
- 🔧 **Instant error fixing** — "column usr_id does not exist" → explains what's wrong and offers the correct query.
- 💬 **Plain English → SQL** — describe what you want, get correct schema-aware SQL.
- 🔒 **Local-first privacy** — runs on your Ollama. Schema and data never leave your machine.

## Why local-first?

Cloud AI SQL assistants send your schema (and sometimes data) to a third party. If you work on sensitive databases, that's a dealbreaker. SQLight runs everything locally through Ollama.
